Option P&L is a simplified model (ATM, ~30 days out, delta, gamma and daily time decay, 3% of equity per trade). It charges 3% of the premium for slippage and $0.65 per contract per side in fees, but uses no historical bid/ask data. It trades the same rule as the live signal: long at agreement +2 or higher, short at −2 or lower, flat otherwise. Volatility here is realized, not implied, so judging real premium needs an options chain feed.

Broker

Orders go to your Alpaca paper account only (see Paper account above). Robinhood is not connected.

Connecting a real account needs a secure backend that holds your credentials and places orders. This page is public front-end code, so it never asks for logins or keys. Robinhood’s official programmatic access is limited, so check their current developer docs before building that layer.

Prices here are randomly generated, not real market data, and every signal and backtest result is illustrative. Options can lose their full premium. This is not financial advice.
